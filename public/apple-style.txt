**Brand Voice & Tone Guidelines: Apple Computers Inc**

**Introduction**
Brand voice and tone guidelines are crucial tools that help maintain consistency across all brand communication. They direct how we express our brand's personality, values and mission through writing. They help team members immerse themselves in the brand, provoking feelings of authenticity, connectedness, and trust in our audience. For Apple Computers Inc, our mission is about "bringing the best user experience to customers through innovative hardware, software, and services." Our guidelines aim to accurately and uniformly reflect this mission.

**Voice & Tone Guiding Principles**

1. **Innovative**: Our voice positions us at the forefront of technology, consistently pioneering new solutions.
2. **Empowering**: We aim to make technology accessible, enhancing our users' experiences and empowering them to unlock their potential.
3. **Authentic**: We are sincere in our communications, upholding transparency and integrity.
4. **Passionate**: We are deeply committed to creating the best electronics and changing lives through technology.

**Guiding Principles Details**

1. **Innovative**
    - **Meaning**: Pioneer spirit, forward-thinking.
    - **How it affects our writing**: We always communicate our commitment to progress.
    - **Best practice copy example**: "Experience the future today with Apple."
    - **What not to do**: Avoid sounding complacent or stagnated.
    - **Incorrect copy example**: "Get your current tech needs met with Apple."

2. **Empowering**
    - **Meaning**: Enabling individuals to do more and become more.
    - **How it affects our writing**: We celebrate the potential our products bring to users.
    - **Best practice copy example**: "Unlock your potential with Apple."
    - **What not to do**: Never belittle our customers' abilities or intelligence.
    - **Incorrect copy example**: "Even you can navigate Apple's complex systems."

3. **Authentic**
    - **Meaning**: Being genuine in our messaging.
    - **How it affects our writing**: Our copy reflects real-world applications and benefits, no hyperbole.
    - **Best practice copy example**: "Apple's reliable technology at your service."
    - **What not to do**: Avoid unfounded claims and exaggerations.
    - **Incorrect copy example**: "Apple will make your wildest dreams come true."

4. **Passionate**
    - **Meaning**: Deeply invested in what we do.
    - **How it affects our writing**: Our excitement, commitment, and zeal shine through our copy.
    - **Best practice copy example**: "We live, breathe, and dream innovation at Apple."
    - **What not to do**: Don't sound detached or apathetic.
    - **Incorrect copy example**: "We make technology. It's just what we do."

**Vocabulary**
Our vocabulary is clear, confident, and approachable, avoiding technical jargon where possible. Instead, we use accessible language that invites and engages even those who are non-tech savvy.

**Tone**
Our tone elicits feelings of excitement, inspiration, and trust. We balance showcasing our top-tier technologies with expressing the joy and potential they bring to individuals' lives.

**Cadence**
The rhythm of our writing is dynamic and energetic, reflecting the fast-paced, innovative world of tech that Apple Computers Inc lives and breathes. Short, punchy sentences are coupled with occasional longer ones to create a balance of quickness and calm.

**Marketing Channels**
* Website copy and blog posts should reflect our authenticity and passion.
* Social media should be a place of excitement and inspiration, heavy on empowering messages.
* Email communication calls for a more personal approach, fostering trust and relationship through individual focus. 
* In product descriptions, strive to evoke the forward-thinking, innovative nature of our brand.
