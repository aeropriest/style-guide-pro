Introduction:
Brand voice and tone guidelines are integral elements that influence how a brand communicates with its audience. They lay down the rules and the stylistic boundaries that ensure consistent communication across all channels. They include reference points on how the brand should sound and feel and how different contexts influence the tone used. The goal of these guidelines is to ensure a uniform communication that reflects the brand's personality and resonates with the target audience. For Google Inc., these guidelines help us deliver our mission of 'organizing the world's information and making it universally accessible and useful.'

Voice & Tone Guiding principles:

1. Informative and Universal
   - Meaning: This is about delivering comprehensive information in an accessible way. Our language should speak to the broadest audience possible. 
   - Effect on writing: Our copy stays light on jargon and heavy on clarity and precision.
   - Correct Copy: "Discover everything you need, with Google Search."
   - Incorrect Copy: "Avail the wide array of information at your fingertips with the realm of Google Search."

2. Empowering and Optimistic 
   - Meaning: We cultivate a voice which is positive and inspires users to take action. 
   - Effect on writing: We create content that is hopeful and encourages users to utilise our tools and features.
   - Correct Copy: "With Google, you’re just a search away from finding whatever you need."
   - Incorrect Copy: "If you want to find something, you could possibly try Google search."

3. Approachable and Friendly 
   - Meaning: As a user-friendly platform, Google's voice should be friendly, approachable, and easy-going.
   - Effect on writing: Our copy combines professionalism with touches of warmth and friendliness.
   - Correct Copy: "Need help? We're here for you, 24/7."
   - Incorrect Copy: "You may contact our support team for assistance."

Vocabulary:
Google's vocabulary is straightforward and clear. We opt for words that are easily understood by a global audience. We value sharing information without cluttering it with complicated words or elaborate metaphors.

Tone: 
The tone is consistently positive, confident, and encouraging. Our copy is a balance of sincerity and authority that fosters trust while maintaining accessibility and user-friendliness.

Cadence: 
The cadence is empathetic, global, and conversational. Short, succinct sentences are preferred to maintain a natural rhythm, akin to everyday speech.

Marketing channels: 
The tone adjusts as per the platform while sticking to the brand voice. For emails/newsletters, the copy is concise, professional, and informative. On social media, the tone becomes more casual and engaging. For blog articles, the tone remains informative, yet with a friendly and conversational touch. The tone for product descriptions combines dynamic and explanatory words to inspire and enlighten the users.
